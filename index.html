Preacher, teacher and author

Apostle Oluwaseun Israel serves as Lead Pastor of Tabernacle of Solace Church in Ibadan, Nigeria, and ministers to churches, leaders and gatherings across the world.

His message is practical and rooted in Scripture, written and spoken for people who are weary, wounded or waiting on God.

Teaching areas: prophetic ministry, deliverance, discipleship, financial wisdom and apostolic authority.
